A flower built around an opening.

The petals gather in uneven layers of turquoise, teal, and deep blue, each one
outlined in black and pulled slightly out of alignment. At the center, a red
geometric iris cuts across a field of cream before giving way to a black pool.
The peace-sign shape is familiar, but the dark center makes it feel less like a
symbol and more like something looking back.

The pile does most of the shading. Short loops catch the light differently as
the petals turn, while the heavy outlines keep the separate pieces legible
from a distance. The red and cream hold the middle open; everything around them
pushes inward.

It lands somewhere between flower, eye, and emblem. Soft material, hard
geometry, and a center that refuses to settle into any of them.
