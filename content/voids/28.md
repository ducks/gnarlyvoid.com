A peace sign built into a flower.

The petals gather in uneven layers of turquoise, teal, and deep blue, each one
outlined in black and pulled slightly out of alignment. At the center, a red
peace symbol sits cleanly inside a cream field. The familiar mark is oversized
enough to become the flower's structure rather than an emblem placed on top.

The pile does most of the shading. Short loops catch the light differently as
the petals turn, while the heavy outlines keep the separate pieces legible
from a distance. The red and cream hold the middle open while the cool petals
push outward around them.

It lands somewhere between flower, badge, and sign. Soft material, hard geometry,
and a symbol that gives the whole thing its rhythm.
